# Code of conduct

Hornero is built by people from different countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want taking part
in the project to be a good experience for everyone.

[Versión en castellano más abajo](#código-de-conducta).

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their
  own. Spanish and English are both welcome.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagreement is fine, personal attacks are not.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and assume good intentions.
- Respect the privacy of others. Never publish someone else's personal data, and never post real customer data
  in issues, logs or screenshots.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and sexual attention or imagery.
- Threats, intimidation or encouraging others to do any of these.
- Spam, trolling or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, write privately to the maintainers at hornero@neatech.ar. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may warn,
suspend or ban the people responsible, temporarily or permanently.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Código de conducta

En Hornero participan personas de distintos países, orígenes y niveles de experiencia, y queremos que sea una
buena experiencia para todas.

- **Esperamos** respeto y paciencia, sobre todo con quien recién llega o escribe en un idioma que no es el suyo,
  discusiones sobre ideas y no sobre personas, críticas constructivas y cuidado de la privacidad: nunca publiques
  datos personales de otros ni datos reales de clientes.
- **No aceptamos** acoso, insultos, chistes o lenguaje discriminatorio, atención o imágenes sexuales, amenazas,
  spam ni conductas que serían inapropiadas en un ámbito profesional.
- **Alcance.** Vale en el repositorio, los issues, los pedidos de cambio, las discusiones y cualquier canal donde
  alguien represente al proyecto.
- **Cómo reportar.** Escribí en privado a los mantenedores a hornero@neatech.ar. Los reportes se tratan con reserva, y
  los mantenedores pueden editar o quitar aportes, advertir, suspender o expulsar a quien no respete este código.
